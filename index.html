Her Story

Small spine.
Giant heart.

Rescued in 2019 by her human, Anna Marie, Tilly was given three to five months to live. She had other plans — and a whole internet to charm along the way.

01 / CONDITION

Short Spine Syndrome

An exceptionally rare condition that compresses the vertebrae — giving Tilly her signature no-neck, loaf-shaped silhouette. It looks unusual, but it causes her no pain at all.

02 / THE ODDS
3 → 7

Months to live became years of thriving. Tilly just celebrated her seventh birthday — healthy, happy, and zooming.

03 / FAME

An internet icon

Millions have fallen for the friendly loaf. She jumps, fetches, and greets everyone with unfiltered joy.

04 / THE NAME

Why "the loaf"?

No neck. Short back. Maximum fluff. Tilly is, quite literally, shaped like a warm loaf of bread.

05 / THE HEART

Different, not broken

Tilly doesn't know she's any different — and that's exactly the point we want the world to feel.
